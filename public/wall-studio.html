How it works

Two Home pages side by side, on the phone's real grid (12 columns, 390 × 844). Grey boxes are the widget, icons and Dock; keep the busy parts out from under them.

The orange dashed line: on a shorter phone (about 700 px tall, Safari with its bars) the icons reach down to there.

Tapping Snake starts the game from the head and the body cells on page 1, so the head goes on page 1 and the body should be one connected line near it.
