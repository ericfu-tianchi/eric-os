3 · Personality firstThe photo is small; the type is big. Four sliders say more than a selfie, and the three reasons explain the match in plain words.

Why a table

The research said 1-on-1 chats are awkward and swiping feels like dating. A table of four is the product's whole thesis, so the main screen is literally that: four seats, one empty.

Color = person

The four logo dots become four seat colors. In the group, a color tells you who's talking faster than a name, and the brand shows up in every screen without a single logo.

Warm, not dating

Paper-white background, ink-black actions and soft tints instead of saturated gradients and full-bleed selfies: it should feel like a café table, not a nightclub.
