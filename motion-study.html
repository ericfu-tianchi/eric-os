Loading and Screen Savers

Every card runs live. Click a preview to see it full screen (click or Esc to leave). Use Pick on the ones you like; the bar at the bottom collects them so you can paste them back.

Loading screen

Shown once per visit, about two seconds, click to skip. Each one has the F mark and the Tianchi OS name, set in Geist like the menubar. They loop here so you can watch them more than once.

Screen savers

Starts after a few idle minutes; any mouse move or key ends it. The ones you pick become choices in Settings. Clock speeds up in the small preview so you can see the minute change.
